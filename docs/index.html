I’m a researcher and head of the Ethnology, Everyday Culture & Society (EECS) department at the Meertens Institute of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences, and Professor of Computational Methods for the Study of Cultural Change at Utrecht University. My research addresses questions about how culture spreads, develops, and disappears, drawing on computational and quantitative methods from ecology, population genetics, and machine learning, with particular attention to methodological challenges in analyzing biased historical collections and measuring cultural diversity and loss.

A vibecoded playground

Games, maps and tools I vibecoded out of cultural heritage collections. Every tale, motif, card and song book you meet in them is a real record from the archive.

Game · 12 days · Volksverhalenbank Sagenland Twelve days in the field, and what the archive kept Cycle the villages of a region that has been telling itself stories for eight years before you arrive. Visit tellers, follow tips, and race the clock: every tale in the game is a real record from the Dutch Folktale Database. Set out Game · 427 tellings · 1781–2015 Roodkapje The Forest of Tellings Walk the forest as Little Red Riding Hood. Every fork in the path is a variant attested in 427 Dutch tellings, from 1781 to 2015, and the real words of those books keep you company along the way. Begin the tale Puzzle game · 120 nights · Volksverhalenbank Nachtdienst Night duty in the Centraal Sagenarchief, 1983 The collector has gone home and left a note on the reading table: twelve envelopes to file before midnight, and cabinet five stays shut. Every puzzle is built from the 9,659 stories of the Dutch Folktale Database.
